## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an ultra-luxury automotive editorial experience tailored for prestigious vehicle showcases. Targeted at discerning collectors, automotive connoisseurs, and high-net-worth clients, the interface cultivates an atmosphere of exclusivity, technical mastery, and sensual elegance.

The visual direction unites high-fashion editorial minimalism with automotive poise:
- **Atmospheric Chiaroscuro:** Deep, theatrical dark environments built from midnight obsidian and cellar burgundies, punctuated by soft directional crimson backlighting and chrome specular highlights.
- **Editorial Poise:** Cinematic scale, generous negative space, asymmetric layouts, and haute-couture proportions that evoke high-end print monographs.
- **Precision Engineering:** Monoline silver borders, razor-sharp hairline dividers, and refined geometric data blocks echoing the tactile precision of automotive coachbuilding.

## Colors

The chromatic architecture rests on a chiaroscuro palette:

- **Primary (`#9B111E` - Carmine Ruby):** An intense, seductive carmine red drawn directly from rich metallic vehicle paint. Reserved for high-priority interactions, subtle focus rings, accent glow gradients, and badge highlights.
- **Secondary (`#E5E9EC` - Liquid Chrome / Platinum):** A polished, luminescent platinum white. Provides supreme legibility for headlines, key specifications, active indicators, and specular rim lighting.
- **Tertiary (`#4A0E17` - Deep Wine Burgundy):** A rich, brooding burgundy establishing atmospheric depth, serving as ambient glow falloffs, secondary card backdrops, and muted interactive states.
- **Neutral (`#09090B` - Obsidian Charcoal):** An ultra-deep near-black canvas that allows vibrant reds and chrome metallics to radiate without visual fatigue. Supported by elevated obsidian layers (`#121316` and `#1A1C20`).
- **Supportive Hairline Halos:** Hairline accents utilize white and platinum with soft opacity values (`rgba(229, 233, 236, 0.12)`) and crimson luminescence (`rgba(155, 17, 30, 0.25)`).

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy establishes tension between classic editorial grace and hyper-modern automotive precision:

- **Serif Display Hierarchy (Playfair Display):** Conveys timeless prestige, heritage, and tailored luxury. Headings should be styled with high breathing room, uncrowded line breaks, and delicate weight nuances.
- **Sans Modern Hierarchy (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Handles technical readouts, narrative copy, navigation, and telemetry data. It offers supreme clarity at small scales, geometric neutrality, and modern ergonomics.
- **Micro-Copy & Metrics:** All uppercase category eyebrow markers, engine specifications, and telemetry titles utilize `label-caps` with wide letter-spacing (`0.18em`) to mirror premium watchmaking and instrument dials.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a 12-column fluid grid system that adheres to haute-couture editorial pacing:

- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns with `gutter` (32px) and generous outer `margin` (64px). Asymmetrical spans (such as 7-column hero vehicle stages paired with 5-column technical dossiers) provide editorial rhythm. Full-bleed backdrop sections host vehicles breaking column bounds.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns with 24px gutters and 32px canvas margins. Dossiers stack into full-width horizontal banners.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns with `gutter-mobile` (16px) and `margin-mobile` (20px). Touch targets remain spacious, with horizontal snap-scroll rails for configuration trims and specifications.
- **Rhythmic Philosophy:** Generous vertical pacing using `space-xl` enforces calm confidence, preventing technical specs from crowding hero photography.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth avoids generic drop shadows, relying instead on theatrical illumination and calibrated surface transparencies:

- **Atmospheric Crimson Glow:** Diffused backlight radiance behind hero assets, created with radial gradients: `radial-gradient(ellipse at center, rgba(155, 17, 30, 0.35) 0%, rgba(74, 14, 23, 0.12) 55%, transparent 75%)`.
- **Specular Glass Surfaces:** Elevated floating overlays, telemetry panels, and navigation bars employ deep glass: dark charcoal fills (`rgba(14, 15, 18, 0.72)`) paired with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)` and subtle noise grain.
- **1px Silver & Chrome Hairlines:** Panels are defined by ultra-fine, translucent perimeter strokes: `border: 1px solid rgba(229, 233, 236, 0.12)`. Active or featured tiers upgrade to an ambient ruby edge: `border: 1px solid rgba(155, 17, 30, 0.45)`.
- **Mirror Reflection Grounds:** Vehicle presentation stages utilize mirror-finish ground gradients, fading vehicle reflections downward into obsidian abyss (`#09090B`).

## Shapes

This design system embraces a **Sharp (`0`)** architecture to reflect precision diamond-cut automotive detailing, structural architecture, and high-fashion gallery framing:

- Interactive elements, modal cards, image viewports, and spec tags feature crisp 90-degree corners (`0px` border radius).
- The uncompromising rectilinear discipline imparts an assertive, gallery-grade confidence, distinguishing the UI from soft, consumer-grade software applications.
- Circular treatment is strictly reserved for the iconic automotive marque, steering controls, and 360-degree rotation compass nodes.

## Components

### Primary & Secondary Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Monolithic sharp rectangle with solid platinum white fill (`#E5E9EC`), obsidian typography (`#09090B`), uppercase `label-caps`. Hover states invoke a fluid carmine red fill (`#9B111E`) with crisp platinum text and a subtle crimson halo (`box-shadow: 0 0 24px rgba(155, 17, 30, 0.45)`).
- **Secondary / Ghost Button:** Transparent background enclosed by a 1px platinum hairline border (`rgba(229, 233, 236, 0.25)`), white text. Hover transitions to `rgba(229, 233, 236, 0.08)` surface fill and a solid chrome border (`#E5E9EC`).

### Automotive Specification Chips & Tags
- Rectilinear low-height tags with obsidian glass background (`rgba(22, 23, 27, 0.6)`), 1px border (`rgba(229, 233, 236, 0.1)`), and `label-caps` typography.
- Active configuration chips incorporate a carmine accent indicator pip (2px square) or full ruby hairline stroke.

### Telemetry & Technical Specification Cards
- Dark obsidian base panels layered with faint vertical hairline dividers (`rgba(229, 233, 236, 0.08)`).
- Hero spec figures (e.g., horsepower, acceleration, torque) rendered in high-contrast `headline-lg` serif or sans, anchored by uppercase descriptive labels beneath.

### Interactive Configurator Pickers & Radios
- Exterior paint and interior hide selectors appear as circular or bevel-cornered swatches nestled within sharp hairline bounds.
- Selected state features a floating 1px carmine ruby ring with an outer 4px dark gap, communicating bespoke bespoke craftsmanship.

### Editorial Narrative & Feature Lists
- Numbers styled in delicate `headline-sm` Playfair Display italics (`01`, `02`, `03`).
- Hairline rules (`1px solid rgba(229, 233, 236, 0.1)`) delineate each item, expanding slightly with ruby accents upon hover.

### Text Inputs & Inquiries
- Flat bottom-line border or ultra-minimal sharp rectangular inputs with obsidian glass fills.
- Active states transition the bottom border from charcoal platinum (`rgba(229, 233, 236, 0.2)`) to brilliant crimson ruby (`#9B111E`), accompanied by an ultra-subtle baseline glow.
